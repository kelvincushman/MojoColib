### Unicode-derived tokenizer tables

`engine/colibri/c/tok_unicode.h` contains category ranges generated by
`engine/colibri/c/tools/gen_unicode.py` from Python's `unicodedata` tables. An
exact reconstruction against the Unicode Character Database identifies the
source data as Unicode 15.0.0. The data is distributed under the Unicode
License v3; its copyright and permission notice is retained at
[`licenses/Unicode-3.0.txt`](licenses/Unicode-3.0.txt).

## Models, datasets and vendor software

The repository's tiny training fixture is generated locally from synthetic
text and deterministic synthetic weights. It is not derived from Qwen or
another downloaded model. Real model weights, tokenizers, datasets, CUDA,
ROCm, Metal support files and device drivers are not relicensed by MojoColib;
each requires a separate approval recorded against the exact revision.
